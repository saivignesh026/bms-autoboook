What Start does

Checks your Discord connection → watches the chosen seats → selects available seats → continues to checkout → fills this profile’s details → opens Scan QR code → sends the selected-seat image and payment QR to Discord.

The page shows a check countdown every half-second and the next reload, between 65 and 140 seconds. You complete payment yourself.
